News

Recent papers, awards, and group announcements.

Aug 2026

Oxide semiconductor density of states

Collaboration on size-dependent band-tail localization in oxide semiconductors, with group members Aravindh Shankar and Sanjeev Khare, posted as arXiv:2608.09283.

Jun 2026

Twisted XY magnets

Noncollinear spin textures and 90° domain walls in twisted XY magnets, with Shiva T. Konakanchi and Sanjeev Khare among the authors: arXiv:2606.00604.

2026

Boron-vacancy T1 at sub-terahertz frequencies

Nano Letters reports spin-relaxation dynamics of VB− centers in hBN, with Abhishek Solanki, Priyo Adhikary, Aravindh Shankar, and collaborators at ORNL and Purdue.

2025

Physical Review Letters on octupole p-bits

Electrically tunable picosecond-scale octupole fluctuations in chiral antiferromagnets (Konakanchi, Banerjee, et al.), opening a path to ultra-fast probabilistic computing.

May 2025

Synaptic plasticity from p-bits

Banerjee, Konakanchi, Datta, and Upadhyaya describe emergent synaptic plasticity from tunable probabilistic-bit dynamics: arXiv:2505.00252.

2023

Skyrmions for Majorana braiding

Physical Review Research: a platform for braiding Majorana modes with magnetic skyrmions (Konakanchi, Väyrynen, Chen, Upadhyaya, Rokhinson).

2020

NSF CAREER

Prof. Upadhyaya receives an NSF CAREER award for spin–magnon based hybrid quantum devices (ECCS-1944635).
