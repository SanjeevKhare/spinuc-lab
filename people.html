Principal investigator

Pramey Upadhyaya

Associate Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering
Area chair, Microelectronics and Nanotechnology
Campus: West Lafayette · Office: WANG 3065
Phone: +1 765-494-5248 · prameyup@purdue.edu

B.Tech, IIT Kharagpur (2009) · M.S. EE, UCLA (2011) · Ph.D., UCLA (2015). Postdoctoral scholar in Physics & Astronomy at UCLA with Yaroslav Tserkovnyak; Ph.D. resident theorist in Kang Wang’s Device Research Laboratory. Honors include an NSF CAREER award (2020), Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship (2013), Intel Summer Fellowship (2011), LANL Director’s Fellowship (2017), and a Purdue Outstanding Engineering Teacher Award.
